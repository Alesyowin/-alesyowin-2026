Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I take part in a competition on www.gpcompetition.com?
Getting started is straightforward. Here is what you need to do:

Go to www.gpcompetition.com and browse the available competitions to find one you would like to enter.
Take a moment to read through the competition details and provide your answer to the qualifying question.
Choose how many tickets you would like to purchase.
Sign in to your existing account, or register for a new one if this is your first time.
Proceed through the checkout, review the Terms & Conditions, and confirm your purchase.

As soon as your order has been successfully processed, you will receive an email confirming that your entry has been received.

Where can I find my confirmation email and ticket numbers?
After your purchase goes through, a confirmation email will be sent to the address registered on your account. This email will include all the details of your entry along with your assigned ticket numbers. Under normal circumstances, this email arrives within 10 to 15 minutes of completing your purchase. During periods of high activity on the site, delivery may take a little longer, so please allow some extra time before checking your inbox or spam folder.
